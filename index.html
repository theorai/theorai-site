The studio

A new lens on the world.

Theorai comes from theoria, the Greek word for looking closely and thinking about what you see. We use AI the same way: to notice what matters in everyday life and turn it into software people rely on.

Every product we make is built around AI from the start, and every one is built to use as little of your data as it can.

How we build.

Three rules every Theorai product follows, from the first prototype to the millionth user.

Only what we need.

Each product starts from the smallest set of data that lets it work. If a feature needs more, we question the feature before we ask for the data.

AI at the core.

Every product is designed around AI from the first line of code. It is how the product works, so it is never an add-on or a badge.

Theory, then life.

We start with a question about how people live, test it with models, and ship only what holds up in everyday use.
